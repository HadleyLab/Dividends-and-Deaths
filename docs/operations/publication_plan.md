# Publication Plan

This plan focuses on editorial convergence, structural tightening, and execution discipline. Use it to move from complete system to publication-ready book.

## Current State
You already have:
- A compelling thesis
- A distinctive authorial voice
- A coherent macro-structure (Parts I-VI)
- Two anchor chapters (Prologue and Chapter 1)
- A proposal-ready foundation

You do not need:
- More ideas, chapters, or concepts
- More personal history

You do need:
- Cohesion enforcement
- Redundancy pruning
- Conceptual load management
- Editorial finish passes

## Governance Check
Ensure these three control documents exist and stay authoritative:
1. Book Spine - narrative contracts and red lines.
2. Concept and Vocabulary Canon - definitions, terms, and reuse phrases.
3. Emotional Arc Map - grief limits, agency return points, and tonal guidance.

## Three Editorial Passes
Pass 1 - Structural Integrity
- Ensure each chapter earns its existence.
- Identify sections to cut or move.
- Avoid line-level edits.

Pass 2 - Concept Compression
- Collapse repeated explanations of codes, bias, and extraction.
- Replace repetition with explicit callbacks.
- Convert explanations into reusable metaphors.

Pass 3 - Language and Music
- Shorten high-intensity sentences.
- Reserve long sentences for reflection.
- Remove grant-style phrasing.

## Reader Target
Pick one ideal reader. Recommended: technically literate lay reader with moral seriousness. This choice controls depth, pacing, and explanation.

## Lock the Ending
Freeze the emotional destination now: agency reclaimed. Every late chapter must move toward custody, participation, governance, and dividends returned.

## External Reality Check
Before querying:
- Share Part I + Prologue with one clinician and one non-medical reader.
- Ask only: "At what point did you feel overwhelmed or confused?"

## Do Not Do
- Do not rewrite Prologue or Chapter 1.
- Do not add more autobiography.
- Do not add more citations to prove authority.
- Do not explain AI or racism basics.
