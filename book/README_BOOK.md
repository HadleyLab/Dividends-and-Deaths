# Dividends & Deaths — Book Governance

## Purpose
Define the invariants that must remain stable across drafts so the book can scale without meaning drift.

## Scope
Applies to all chapters, concepts, protocols, and editorial passes from **Prologue through Epilogue**.

Nothing in `book/` is optional.
Nothing here is illustrative.

## Related Documents
- Root context and directory map: `README.md`
- Canonical ontology: `concepts/README_CONCEPTS.md`
- Execution protocols: `protocols/README_PROTOCOLS.md`
- Governed prose bundles: `chapters/README_CHAPTERS.md`

---

## Non-Negotiables

- Governance documents override prose.
- Death is never treated as accidental or mysterious.
- Grief is permitted only after structure is explicit.
- Every chapter must trace harm to engine failure.

If a chapter "feels right" but violates these rules, it is wrong.

## Update Triggers

- a governance invariant changes
- authority order changes
- a governance document is added or removed

---

## How This Book Is Written (Symbiotic Human Computation)

Two orthogonal inputs:
- Lived episodes & cases (witness)
- Formal concept construction (structure)

These inputs are reconciled through **governance and ledgers**, not intuition.

AI is used to test consistency, surface contradictions, compress language, and enforce reuse.
AI does **not** supply authority, witness, or moral judgment.

---

## Canonical Production Flow

Episodes / Cases ↔ Concepts  
→ Narrative Assets (ledgered)  
→ Chapter Bundles (`chapter_NN_prose.md` + `chapter_NN_ledger.md` + `chapter_NN_scene.md`)  
→ Book-Level Enforcement (spine, emotional arc, red lines)

Key invariants:
- **Ledger precedes prose**
- **Concepts precede reuse**
- **Governance overrides style**

---

## Digital Māyā and Ma’at (Operational Balance)

- **Digital Māyā**: representations begin to govern action.
- **Ma’at**: enforceable constraint binding representation to consequence.

Māyā is never eliminated.
Ma’at is never optional.

---

## Authority Map (Read This When Confused)

- `overview.md` — what the book claims and how parts fit together
- `spine.md` — narrative contracts, red lines, and throughline
- `emotional_arc.md` — when grief is allowed and how agency returns
- `publication_plan.md` — how drafts converge into a finished book

When conflicts arise:
1. Spine overrides chapters
2. Emotional arc overrides tone
3. Governance overrides elegance

---

## Author–System Contract

- The author supplies meaning, witness, and responsibility.
- The system supplies structure, memory, and enforcement.
- Neither replaces the other.

Episodes without governance become memoir.
Concepts without episodes become abstraction.
