# Dividends & Deaths - Emotional Arc Map

## Purpose
Control reader fatigue and momentum by governing where the book permits grief, when it demands analysis, and how often agency must return. This document is a guardrail, not prose. This map enforces the book’s death-bound framework: grief is permitted only when it advances structural accountability, and analysis must always trace mortality to engine failure.

## Scope
Applies to every chapter, with special attention to the Prologue and Part transitions.

## Emotional Temperature by Part
| Section | Emotional Temperature | Dominant Emotions | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prologue | High shock, high intimacy | Awe, grief, indignation | Open with Henrietta + family history; establish death as extraction’s origin; do not resolve grief here. |
| Part I - The Economy We Inherited | Controlled intensity | Curiosity, unease | Teach geometry and engines; minimal personal grief; reader should see how death becomes an audit. |
| Part II - Extraction | Rising anger | Betrayal, frustration | Allow brief grief spikes, but each must close with a named engine failure. |
| Part III - Circular Economy | Intellectual burn | Alarm, urgency | No grief; prove inevitability and legal asymmetry; show friction removal between error and death. |
| Part IV - Rewriting the Economy | Hope rising | Determination, relief | Introduce Ma’at as governance once failure can kill; grief only as resolved memory. |
| Part V - Patient-Centered Future | Sustained agency | Resolve, curiosity | Readers must feel part of a movement; grief prohibited. |
| Part VI - The Future We Deserve | Quiet conviction | Commitment, responsibility | Tone is solemn but empowered; final note is custody returned, not catharsis. |

## Grief and Intimacy Allotment
- Max grief scenes: 4 (Prologue, Chapter 3, Part II close, one flashback in Part IV)
- No grief zones: Part III Chapter 2 onward, entire Part V, Part VI
- Personal medical stories: never back-to-back; at least one analytical chapter between them
- Family references after Part II: only as callbacks ("As my father's pacemaker taught me..."), not new scenes
- Grief is never standalone; every grief scene must terminate in a structural or engine-level cause.

## Tonal Guardrails
- Never stack despair paragraphs without a systems fact in between.
- Use first-person only when delivering witness testimony; default to analyst voice elsewhere.
- After every data-heavy section, insert a single sentence that restates the human consequence.
- After every grief vignette, close with one line that names the structural cause.
- Death is never framed as tragedy without mechanism; mortality must be legible as system output.

## Recovery Valves (reader breathers)
- Short interludes (<=150 words) after Chapters 2, 5, and 8 that summarize "What we know so far."
- Visual artifacts (tables, diagrams) introduced only when emotional temperature is low to avoid overload.
- Sidebars labeled "Law We Already Have" to convert outrage into comprehension.

## Monitoring Checklist (per chapter review)
- Where does this chapter sit on the temperature ladder? Does the prose match?
- Did I exceed the allotted grief quota?
- Where does agency return in this chapter? If nowhere, is that intentional?
- Does the closing paragraph hand the reader to the next part with the correct emotional state?

---

## Canonical Emotional Contract

The reader is allowed to grieve only when the book has earned the right through structure.

Emotion follows mechanism, never the reverse.

If a chapter cannot explain *why* a death occurred in system terms, it does not yet deserve to ask the reader to feel it.
