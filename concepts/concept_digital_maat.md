# Digital Ma’at

## Purpose
Define Ma’at rendered operational in modern data systems.

## Scope
Applies to passages describing enforceable governance in healthcare data.

## Category
Governance Construct

## Canonical Definition
Digital Ma’at is the operationalized governance condition that aligns representation with consequence through enforceable custody, provenance, and accountability.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when describing governance mechanisms that bind value to responsibility.
- Treat as structural enforcement, not sentiment or ethics.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Ma’at (Egyptian Concept):** the philosophical anchor.
- **Equitable Governance Layer:** the institutional mechanism.
- **Open Provenance Token System:** the infrastructural enforcement.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Governance is Ma’at rendered operational.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
Without enforceable governance, representation drifts and errors persist.  
That drift becomes systemic harm and preventable death.
