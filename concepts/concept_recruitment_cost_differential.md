# Recruitment Cost Differential

## Purpose
Define the price gap that makes representation economically penalized.

## Scope
Applies to passages describing recruitment economics and exclusion incentives.

## Category
Policy Context

## Canonical Definition
Recruitment cost differential is the gap in cost required to recruit participants from different populations, often making inclusion economically disfavored.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when describing advertising, outreach, or trial enrollment economics.
- Tie the differential to incentives that reduce representational breadth.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Underrepresentation in Trials:** cost gaps drive exclusion.
- **Discovery Engine:** recruitment costs shape who becomes evidence.
- **Mortality Gap:** exclusion at recruitment becomes downstream harm.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“The market prices representation—and punishes it.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
When representation is priced as a premium, systems select for the cheapest evidence.  
That exclusion is later paid in mortality.
