# Provenance

## Purpose
Define traceable lineage of data from human origin to downstream use.

## Scope
Applies to chapters that discuss data rights, consent, or governance.

## Category
Governance Construct

## Canonical Definition
Provenance is the traceable lineage of data from human origin to downstream use, including consent, transformation, reuse, and value generation.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when describing auditability, consent, or correction.
- Treat as a right, not a feature.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Custody:** provenance enables custody to be enforced.
- **Information Engine:** provenance constrains how representations travel.
- **Ma’at:** provenance is governance made operational.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Provenance is a right, not a feature.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
Without provenance, errors persist across institutions and time.  
That persistence delays correction and can convert misrepresentation into mortality.
