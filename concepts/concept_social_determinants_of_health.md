# Social Determinants of Health

## Purpose
Define the social conditions that shape clinical outcomes.

## Scope
Applies to passages describing housing, income, education, food access, and safety.

## Category
Policy Context

## Canonical Definition
Social determinants of health are the material and social conditions that shape risk, illness, and recovery.  
They are not ancillary to care; they are causal.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when describing non-clinical drivers of outcomes.
- Tie to data completeness and legibility failures.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Data Completeness:** social context is often missing in records.
- **Access:** social conditions determine whether care can occur.
- **Mortality Gap:** unequal conditions produce unequal survival.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Social conditions are clinical conditions.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
When social determinants are ignored, clinical decisions are built on partial truth.  
That partial truth leads to mistreatment and preventable death.
