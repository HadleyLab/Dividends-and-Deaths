# Comorbidity

## Purpose
Define the presence of overlapping conditions as a structural risk multiplier.

## Scope
Applies to chapters that describe multi-condition risk and model failure.

## Category
System Primitive

## Canonical Definition
Comorbidity is the presence of multiple concurrent conditions that interact to reshape risk and care trajectories.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when explaining why single-disease models fail in real populations.
- Tie comorbidity to representation quality and care coordination.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Data Completeness:** comorbidity requires full records to be legible.
- **Three Engines:** discovery and care fail when conditions are modeled in isolation.
- **Mortality as Ledger:** multiplicative risk surfaces as death gaps.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Comorbidity turns additive risk into multiplicative risk.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
When comorbidity is ignored, risk scores understate danger and care is delayed.  
The result is preventable mortality in the populations least represented in data.
