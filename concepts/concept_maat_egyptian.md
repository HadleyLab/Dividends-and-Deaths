# Ma’at (Egyptian Concept)

## Purpose
Define Ma’at as the original Egyptian concept that anchors the book’s governance logic.

## Scope
Applies to passages naming Ma’at explicitly as a historical and philosophical reference.

## Category
Lens

## Canonical Definition
Ma’at is the ancient Egyptian concept of order, balance, truth, and rightful accounting.
In this book it is a framing anchor, not a religious or moral instruction.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use only when explicitly naming the Egyptian concept.
- Translate immediately into structural language (custody, provenance, enforcement).
- Do not treat as spirituality or cultural ornament.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Digital Ma’at:** the operationalized form in modern systems.
- **Digital Māyā:** the illusion Ma’at must constrain.
- **Equitable Governance Layer:** where Ma’at becomes enforceable.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Ma’at names order, balance, and accountable accounting.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
When order and accountability are absent, representation drifts without consequence.  
That drift scales into preventable harm and death.
