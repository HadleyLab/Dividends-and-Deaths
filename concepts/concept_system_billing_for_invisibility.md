# System Billing for Invisibility

## Purpose
Define the incentive structure that rewards systems even when people are unseen.

## Scope
Applies to passages describing reimbursement that persists despite missingness or misrepresentation.

## Category
Lens

## Canonical Definition
System billing for invisibility is the condition where institutions capture value even when people are absent from records, misclassified, or excluded from evidence.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when describing how reimbursement survives failure of representation.
- Tie to data completeness and structural underrepresentation.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Billable Code:** billing does not require full representation.
- **Data Completeness:** missing context does not stop payment.
- **Underrepresentation in Trials:** invisibility is compatible with revenue.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“The system bills for what it cannot see.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
When invisibility is compatible with revenue, missingness persists.  
That persistence leads to misdiagnosis and avoidable death.
