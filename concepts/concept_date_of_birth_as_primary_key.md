# Date of Birth as Primary Key

## Purpose
Define identity-as-index in the American medical system.

## Scope
Applies to passages describing how systems identify, match, or govern people.

## Category
System Primitive

## Canonical Definition
Date of birth as primary key is the system practice of using a birthdate as the core identifier for record matching, eligibility, and consent.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when describing how legibility is established by fields, not memory.
- Frame as a structural choice, not a natural law.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Legibility:** primary keys are the admission ticket into a record system.
- **Billable Code:** primary keys anchor billing and reimbursement.
- **Custody:** identity fields determine who can act on a record.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“In American medicine, the date of birth is the primary key.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
When identity depends on fragile keys, mismatches and omissions compound.  
Those errors delay care and can become fatal.
