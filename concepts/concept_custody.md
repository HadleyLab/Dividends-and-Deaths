# Custody

## Purpose
Define who controls, governs, and can act on representation.

## Scope
Applies to any passage describing control of data, codes, or identity.

## Category
System Primitive

## Canonical Definition
Custody is who controls, governs, and can act on a representation.
The central failure of modern healthcare and AI is misassigned custody.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when describing who holds decision power over representation.
- Tie custody to enforceable rights, not goodwill.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Provenance:** tracks lineage; custody determines who can act on it.
- **Digital Māyā:** representation governs action when custody is misassigned.
- **Ma’at:** governance is custody made enforceable.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Custody, not capability, is the core problem.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
When custody is misassigned, errors cannot be corrected by the people harmed.  
Those errors persist across encounters and can become fatal.
