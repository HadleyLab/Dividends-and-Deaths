# Extraction

## Purpose
Define extraction as the conversion of human life into symbols for profit without custody.

## Scope
Applies to chapters describing consent failure, value capture, or representational imbalance.

## Category
System Primitive

## Canonical Definition
Extraction is the conversion of human life into symbols for profit without consent, custody, or compensation.  
It is distinct from ethical research, which requires governance.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when value is captured without custodial accountability.
- Do not conflate extraction with innovation or discovery.
- Tie extraction to a specific engine failure when harm is implied.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Billable Code:** the symbolic unit extraction monetizes.
- **Digital Māyā:** representation enables extraction without custody.
- **Custody / Provenance:** the governance mechanisms extraction bypasses.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Extraction converts representation into profit without custody.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
Extraction becomes lethal when it removes incentives to correct error.  
Without custody, representational failure persists and mortality becomes the system’s only audit.
