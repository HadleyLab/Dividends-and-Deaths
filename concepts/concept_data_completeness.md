# Data Completeness

## Purpose
Define the integrity of a record as a prerequisite for safe care.

## Scope
Applies to chapters that describe missingness, fragmentation, or loss of context.

## Category
System Primitive

## Canonical Definition
Data completeness is the degree to which a record captures the full clinical and social context needed for accurate action.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when describing missingness, fragmented records, or invisible risk.
- Treat missingness as structural, not random.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Legibility:** completeness determines who can be seen.
- **Information Engine:** incomplete records compound across systems.
- **Comorbidity:** incomplete records erase overlapping risk.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Missingness is not random; it is structural.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
Incomplete data hides risk, delays intervention, and misdirects care.  
Those delays translate directly into preventable deaths.
