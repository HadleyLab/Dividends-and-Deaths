# Dividends and Deaths

## Purpose
Define the book’s accounting framework for value and harm.

## Scope
Applies to any chapter that contrasts economic gain with mortality outcomes.

## Category
Lens

## Canonical Definition
Dividends and Deaths is the book’s paired accounting framework: dividends flow upward to institutions, and deaths fall downward on those excluded or misrepresented.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Always use as a paired phrase; do not reference one without the other.
- Use when translating system incentives into human consequence.
- Avoid treating the phrase as metaphor only; it is a ledger.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Precision Medicine Economy:** the system that generates both outputs.
- **Billable Code:** the unit that enables dividend extraction.
- **Mortality as Ledger:** death is the final audit.
- **Extraction:** the mechanism that drives asymmetry.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Dividends rise. Deaths fall.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
When representation and custody fail, dividends continue to accumulate while mortality concentrates in underrepresented populations.  
Deaths are the measurable consequence of misgoverned extraction.
