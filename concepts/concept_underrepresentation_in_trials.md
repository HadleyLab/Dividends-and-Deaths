# Underrepresentation in Trials

## Purpose
Define exclusion from clinical trials as a discovery failure.

## Scope
Applies to passages about trial enrollment, eligibility, and downstream harm.

## Category
Policy Context

## Canonical Definition
Underrepresentation in trials is the structural exclusion of populations from clinical research, producing therapies optimized for others.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when discussing eligibility rules, recruitment barriers, or trial geography.
- Tie to discovery engine failure and downstream care inequity.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Discovery Engine:** exclusion at trial stage produces future harm.
- **Mortality Gap:** trial bias widens survival differences.
- **Recruitment Cost Differential:** cost incentives drive exclusion.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Trial underrepresentation is future death baked into discovery.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
When trials exclude the most vulnerable, therapies are less effective where need is greatest.  
The resulting care failures produce predictable mortality gaps.
