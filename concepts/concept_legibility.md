# Legibility

## Purpose
Define legibility as the condition for system action.

## Scope
Applies to passages describing representation, inclusion, or absence in data.

## Category
System Primitive

## Canonical Definition
Legibility is the condition of being representable to a system.  
It enables action but can erase context.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when describing who can be seen, measured, or acted on by a system.
- Do not equate legibility with personhood.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Billable Code:** the primary mechanism of clinical legibility.
- **Digital Māyā:** legibility without custody becomes illusion.
- **Three Engines:** legibility determines who enters discovery, care, and information.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Legibility enables action but can erase context.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
When legibility is uneven, care follows the record instead of the body.  
That asymmetry produces delayed diagnosis and preventable mortality.
