# Equitable Governance Layer

## Purpose
Define the governance mechanism that binds representation to consequence.

## Scope
Applies to passages describing custody reassignment and enforceable alignment.

## Category
Governance Construct

## Canonical Definition
An equitable governance layer is the enforceable structure that aligns representation with accountability, so value and harm are both traceable to responsible actors.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when describing governance that must exist once failure can kill.
- Tie to provenance, custody, and audit.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Ma’at:** the governance condition rendered operational.
- **Provenance:** the mechanism that makes enforcement possible.
- **Billable Code:** the unit that must be governed.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Equity requires an enforceable governance layer.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
Without enforceable governance, representation drifts and errors persist.  
That drift becomes mortality when no actor is compelled to correct harm.
