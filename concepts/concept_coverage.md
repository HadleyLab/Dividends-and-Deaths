# Coverage

## Purpose
Define coverage as the authorization layer that gates care.

## Scope
Applies to passages describing reimbursement, eligibility, and authorization.

## Category
Policy Context

## Canonical Definition
Coverage is the authorization layer that determines whether care is reimbursable and therefore possible within the system.

## Usage / Placement Rules
- Use when describing insurance rules, eligibility, or authorization barriers.
- Distinguish coverage from access; coverage is necessary but not sufficient.

## Relationship to Canonical Concepts
- **Access:** coverage is a prerequisite for access in many systems.
- **Billable Code:** coverage is enforced through coding logic.
- **Mortality as Ledger:** coverage failures delay or deny care.

## Reusable Phrase (Canonical)
“Coverage gates care before it reaches the clinic.”

## Death-Bound Clarification
When coverage blocks care, diagnosis and treatment are delayed.  
Those delays translate into avoidable harm and death.
